Read the FAQs

We’ve answered the most common ones here.

  • Are you more expensive than freelancers?

    We’re not a volume agency. You pay a fixed price for senior work that’s tested and ready to ship.

  • Why not just hire in-house?

    A full-time AI hire takes months and costs far more. We ship one feature in fourteen days and hand over the code.

  • What does a sprint cost?

    $4,500 fixed for one AI feature. Half to start, half on delivery.

  • Who is Whitestone Labs best for?

    B2B software teams of 5 to 50 people whose customers are asking for AI. And agencies that need AI or 3D work they can’t staff.

  • How involved do we need to be?

    We agree the test cases with you on day one. After that you give feedback and approvals, and we handle the build.

  • What if it doesn’t pass the tests?

    It ships when every agreed case passes. Wrong or unsourced answers are fixed and run again.

  • How fast do you move?

    Fourteen days for an AI feature. Ten days for a launch site.

  • Who will I be working with?

    Directly with Alex, who builds it. No account managers. No handoffs.
